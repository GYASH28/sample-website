## Reference assets

Supplier files are intentionally organized in the repository so a future upload can reuse them without searching a Downloads folder. The original PDFs live in `docs/catalogue/source-shade-cards/`, outside the public website bundle, so they do not slow down customer visits:

- `shade-cards/` holds the supplied PDF catalogues and shade cards.
- `product-photos/` holds selected source product photos that establish the real range.
- `images/editorial/fakhri-real-range-hero.png` is the new generated, label-free catalog hero used on the website; it is representative art, not a pack-label image.

## Quotation checklist

1. Product collection and exact requested shade.
2. Quantity and whether the order is retail or wholesale.
3. Current availability and a current pack/shade photo.
4. Price, dispatch timing, and delivery destination.

Never infer a product's composition, pack weight, needle/hook size or care instructions from this register. Confirm it from the current supplier pack or shade card first.
