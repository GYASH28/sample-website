## Quotation checklist

1. Product collection and exact requested shade.
2. Requested quantity and whether the order is retail or wholesale.
3. Current availability and a current pack/shade photo.
4. Price, dispatch timing, and delivery destination.

Never infer a product's composition, pack weight, needle/hook size, care instructions, sale unit or availability from this register. Confirm it from the current supplier pack, shade card or current stock information first.
