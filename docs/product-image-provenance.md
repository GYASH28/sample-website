# Product image provenance

The 34 current catalogue lines each have a distinct, labelled studio visual. The
full-size files in `public/assets/images/products/verified-web/` retain the
generator's native 1254 × 1254 pixels using lossless WebP encoding. The
720 × 720 `card-web/` derivatives are display-size optimisations; product
detail and zoom views use the native-size file.

These are studio reconstructions, not evidence of stock on hand. Supplier
name and product-line text comes from the customer-provided handover and shade
cards. No price, fibre composition, pack weight or availability was inferred
from an image. Customers should request a live stock photo before ordering.

## Packaging reference tiers

- Pack appearance guided by a supplied photo or catalogue pack illustration:
  Ganga Olivia, Blankie Solid, Blankie Multi and Superstitch; Vardhaman Baby
  Soft and Cotone.
- All other images: the label identifies a confirmed product line, but its
  physical band design is illustrative because no sufficiently clear pack
  reference was available in the provided files. This distinction is stated on
  the product detail page.
- Malai Dori, Macramé Cord, Glacé Cotton Thread Art 545 and Kasab have no
  confirmed manufacturer in the provided handover. Their bands therefore
  carry only a product name or size, not an invented brand.

The original supplier PDFs and photos remain the authority for exact shades,
logos and packaging. Generative reconstruction cannot guarantee every tiny
piece of print on a physical label; confirm those details from a live photo.
